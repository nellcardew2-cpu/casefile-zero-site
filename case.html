Brief · 01

The incident

Halloway called it a document variance. Daniel Wren called it an entry.

At 18:14 on Thursday, 14 November 2024, Records Supervisor Harriet Cole found the blue seal on Archive 4 broken. The corridor camera had gone offline at 18:00 and returned at 18:11. Inside, the Northbridge exception memorandum occupied the correct folder and the correct position.

That was the first thing wrong with it.

The memorandum was dated 14 February 2017. It appeared to authorise a reserve adjustment now being processed by Finance. If genuine, the adjustment was routine. If inserted that evening, somebody had manufactured authority seven years after the event.

Archive 4 has one ordinary badge door and one service door. During the camera outage the ordinary door recorded no badge entry. The service door recorded a manual override. Its emergency key was normally kept in a sealed cabinet inside the Secure Print Room.

Four employees remained relevant. Each gave an account supported by a Halloway timestamp. Unfortunately, Halloway had several timestamps and only one actual time.

The questions When did the service door open? Who used it? What was replaced?

Brief · 03

How the clocks work

Displayed time=Actual time+Offset

Example: an offset of +6 means the system displays six minutes fast.

Rules established by the clock audit

  • The central switchboard clock is the master reference and shows actual time.
  • Each of the five local systems has a fixed offset throughout the incident.
  • When the same event appears on two systems, the seconds match exactly.
  • Audit check only: after deriving them, the five offsets should be different and fall from −6 to +6 minutes, inclusive. Do not use this rule to guess them.
  • A timestamp is not false merely because its clock is wrong.

Level 1

Method only

No offset is revealed.

Find simultaneous pairs. The switchboard and Lift 2 record the same emergency button press. The lift and mail systems record the same reset. The access and mail systems record the same archive-door contact. The access and print systems record the same badge touch.

  1. Use master switchboard time to correct the lift.
  2. Use the lift reset to correct the mail server.
  3. Use the mail alert to correct the access controller.
  4. Use corrected access time to correct the print server.

Keep the barrier separate: it compares directly with the master switchboard.

Verified solution

Why the timeline identifies her

Marcus was outside by 17:58:33, with no recorded return. Simon remained on the fixed Server Room call from 18:00:04 to 18:11:28. Harriet remained trapped until 18:11:40. Priya entered Secure Print at 18:02:20, could take the missing key and reach Archive 4 by 18:05:15. After closing at 18:09:00, the slower return route cleared the corridor by 18:10:45, before the camera resumed.

Why the document identifies the act

The recovered copy's K-24 stock and T3-1114 day mark prove it came from Tray 3 on 14 November, but do not identify a job. Priya's two-page private job matches the pages; the corrected timeline is what places her at Archive 4. The memorandum also named a unit that did not exist until 2019. It was newly made to look old.

Purpose

The adjustment carried both Priya's and Marcus's names. The timeline excludes Marcus and leaves Priya with the access, document and opportunity to manufacture retroactive authority.
