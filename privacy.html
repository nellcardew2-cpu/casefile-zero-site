Privacy Notice

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Who is responsible for your information

This website and the Nell Cardew email list are operated by Catalin Grigoriev, an individual author publishing under the pen name Nell Cardew, based in Italy. Nell Cardew is a pen name and not a separate company.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email nellcardew2@gmail.com. No data protection officer has been appointed.

What we collect and why

When you subscribe, we collect the email address you enter and the subscription information needed to confirm and manage your request. We use this information to:

  • send the confirmation needed to complete your subscription;
  • provide access to Case Zero — The Eleven-Minute Gap as your welcome gift; and
  • send occasional Nell Cardew emails about new books, mystery puzzles and related bonus content.

We process this information to administer the subscription you requested. Our legal basis for sending newsletter and promotional emails is your consent. You give that consent by actively selecting the consent box and completing the confirmation process.

We do not sell your email address or share it for another organisation's marketing.

Brevo and the sign-up form

We use Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France) to provide the sign-up form, manage subscriptions and consent records, maintain unsubscribe records, and send emails. Brevo processes this information on our behalf.

When the Brevo form loads or is submitted, Brevo may process technical information needed to operate and protect the form, such as connection, browser, date, time and IP-address information.

Spam protection

We use Google reCAPTCHA to protect the sign-up form from automated submissions and abuse. When reCAPTCHA runs, Google may process technical, browser and interaction information and set the necessary _GRECAPTCHA cookie for risk analysis. We use this protection on the basis of our legitimate interest in keeping the form and mailing list secure. More information is available in Google's reCAPTCHA FAQ.

Unsubscribing

You can withdraw your consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any newsletter or by emailing nellcardew2@gmail.com. Withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew consent, and it does not remove a PDF you have already downloaded.

The website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub records visitor IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not receive those server logs.

We do not use audience analytics or advertising trackers on this site. The Brevo sign-up form and Google reCAPTCHA are the third-party services described above.

Notes in the interactive case

The Case Board stores your answers and revealed-hint choices in your browser's local storage so that you can return to them on the same device. Those notes are not transmitted to Nell Cardew or Brevo. You can remove them using the board's reset control or your browser settings.

How long we keep information

  • While subscribed: we keep your email address and subscription information for as long as you remain on the list.
  • After unsubscribing: we remove the active subscriber profile within 30 days, except for the limited records described below.
  • Consent and unsubscribe evidence: we retain the minimum information reasonably necessary to demonstrate compliance and resolve related claims.
  • Suppression list: we retain the email address on a do-not-contact list for as long as the list exists, solely to honour the unsubscribe request and prevent accidental re-enrolment.

Your rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may request access to, correction or deletion of your information, restriction of or objection to processing, and portability where applicable. You may withdraw consent at any time. Contact nellcardew2@gmail.com. We normally respond within one month; where the law permits an extension, we will explain why.

You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data-protection authority. In Italy, this is the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Age

This email list is intended for adults and is not directed to anyone under 16. If you are under 16, please do not subscribe.

Changes to this notice

We will update this page and the date above if our practices change. If a change materially affects current subscribers, we will also provide an appropriate notice.
